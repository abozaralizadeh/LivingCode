LivingCode

Let your app fix its own bugs.

A guard with no AI in it puts the last good version back when a fix breaks.

$ livingcode approve c00001-e90107cbf0
Settled: r0003
$ livingcode deploy <lab>/people-change
RolledBack: smoke check http://127.0.0.1:63537/checkout gave 500
$ sandbox-exec (no network) livingcode rollback
RolledBack: now on r0001
